Common Questions

Quick answers before you visit.

What are your clinic timings?

We are open every day from 10 AM to 9 PM. Booking in advance on WhatsApp helps us keep your waiting time short.

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 70202 32838, or call +91 70573 94624. You can also fill the form on this page and it will open WhatsApp with your details.

Do you treat both dental and skin or hair problems?

Yes. Dr. Ishani is a dental surgeon (BDS) with a Diploma in Clinical Cosmetology, so we offer dental care along with hydrafacial, peels, laser hair removal and other skin and hair treatments.

Yes, we offer dental implants, crowns, bridges, dentures, braces and aligners. After an examination, Dr. Ishani will suggest the best option for you.
